# Privacy Policy

[日本語](privacy.ja.md)

The operator of Archieves (the "Service"; the "Operator") handles your information as follows. The Service does not ask for your real name or other information that identifies you. You may use a nickname in the name field. This is a translation for convenience. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

## 1. Information we collect

- Account: the email address you sign in with (kept by the authentication service; the Operator never sees your password)
- Profile: name, nickname, affiliation and visibility settings (all but the name are optional)
- What you record: scores, where your arrows landed, comments and diary entries
- What you send through Feedback: the message and the email address you give for a reply
- Error information: the screen, the request and the error that occurred, and the user who was signed in at the time
- Stored on your device: settings such as language and menu position, and records you are entering but have not sent yet. These stay in your browser and are not sent to the Operator
- Information that keeps you signed in (cookies and tokens)

## 2. How we use it

- to provide the Service (saving records, statistics, rankings and so on)
- to confirm that you are who you say you are
- to reply to Feedback and deliver News
- to fix bugs and improve the Service
- to prevent use that breaks the Terms of Service

We do not use it for any other purpose.

## 3. What other users can see

Your profile and ranking entries are shown to other users according to the visibility you choose (private, people in the same affiliation, or everyone). The default is private.

People who can see your profile also see your name, nickname, affiliation and recent statistics. Leave out anything you do not want others to know.

Rankings show your name and scores.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not give your information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law, or where it is needed to protect someone's life, body or property and it is difficult to obtain your consent.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## 5. Where your information is kept

The Service runs on the cloud of Amazon Web Services, Inc. (AWS). Your information is stored on servers in the AWS Tokyo Region (in Japan). AWS only provides the servers and does not view or use your information.

## 6. Analytics and advertising

The Service currently uses no analytics tools or advertising. If that changes, we will update this policy and let you know.

## 7. Security

We take measures to prevent leaks and loss of information, such as encrypting communication, limiting who has access, and restricting connections to the database.

## 8. Viewing, correcting and deleting your information

You can correct or delete your profile on the Profile Settings screen, and your records on their own screens, at any time. Export on the Settings screen lets you take out all of your records.

You can delete your account and all of your data with "Delete account" on the Settings screen. To request disclosure of your information, contact us through Feedback. We will respond after confirming your identity.

## 9. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. Deleting your account immediately deletes your records, diary entries, comments, feedback and error information along with it. Error information may be deleted once it is no longer needed for fixing bugs.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when needed. For major changes, we will let you know in advance in News or elsewhere.

## 11. Contact

For questions about how your information is handled, contact us through Feedback in the Service.
